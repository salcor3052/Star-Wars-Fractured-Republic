# Gold Skull Pirates

A large pirate clan that operates in the Outer Rim between the galactic powers.

* Inspired by Hondo Onaka’s pirate force, the Gold Skulls are swashbuckling pirates and cutthroats. They’re extravagant, over-the-top adversaries that sail the black seas in search of prey and plunder.  
* They’re smart, but not soft. They’re ruthless, but not merciless.   
  * They’d much rather pillage a starship quickly rather than paint the bulkheads with the blood of the crew and passengers, but they’ll also burn an obstinate captain down to set an example for everyone else.  
  * Bad \- but not eeeeeeeevil...  
* Their starships boldly display the group’s yellow “alien-skull-over-crossed blades” icon with paint that can be detected on standard visual sensors as well as other scanning spectrums.  
  * They are not subtle, and they use their reputation as a weapon.  
* They are led by the Twi’lek pirate, Commodore Vi’le Carnage, as over the top as his name implies.  
  * Carnage is dashing, eloquent, charming, and utterly ruthless if provoked.  
  * He’s a master swordsman and expert marksman with a blaster pistol.   
  * He leads the Gold Skull pirates with a combination of admiration and fear.  
  * And yes he speaks with an *outrageous* Rylothian accent.  
* **How to use the Gold Skull Pirates:** They’re PIRATES\!   
  * They raid ships, space stations, and isolated colonies looking for stuff to steal.   
  * If they score a large amount of loot, they’ll need to transport and sell it on the various black markets around the galaxy.   
  * Rangers could encounter them during any of their operations.  
  * Take your favorite pirate movie, novel, or any Hondo Onaka episode and create your adventure from there.

### Vi'le Carnage

*"I would much rather steal from you than kill you, but we can't always get what we want, no?" \--blaster sound--*

### **Biography**

Leader of the Gold Belt Pirates. Incredibly egotistical, and stylizes himself as the romanticized image of a space pirate captain; dashing, ruthless, charming, suave, and with implacable taste \-- only half of which he's actually able to pull off. Still, Carnage is able to accumulate a sizable following under his banner, large enough that he has proclaimed himself as "Commodore Carnage" ("Not Admiral, Commodore\! Admirals just sit around at desks all day, Commodores are out there getting the job DONE\!")

### **Appearance**

Tall, stocky, and slightly overweight. Blue skin. Lekku is adorned with spots of various sizes. Speaks with an *outrageous* Rylothian accent.
