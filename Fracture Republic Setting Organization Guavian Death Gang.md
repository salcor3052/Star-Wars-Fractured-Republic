# Guavian Death Gang

* Once a crime gang, the Death Gang have evolved into a mercenary shock force. To use a Rifts term, think of them as “Cyborg juicers for hire”.  
  * The members of the Death Gang are heavily modified, combat cyborgs always on the look out for better upgrades as well as opportunities to cut loose with the tech they have at their disposal.  
    * They love cybernetics, heavy weapons, and things that turn matter to ash or make really big “BOOMS”.  
  * The Guavian Death Gang will pretty much do anything with the right amount of money or technological compensation.  
    * If you want a precise, military operation, you hire the Mobile Regiment. When you need the job done quickly and don’t care about massive collateral damage, you hire the Guavian Death Gang.  
  * The Death Gang is also heavily invested in black market, outlaw technologies (especially cybernetics).  
  * **How to use the Guavian Death Gang:** the Guavian Death Gang falls in the Venn Diagram somewhere between the First Sun, the Pike Syndicate, and the Dra’jn, as far as situations the Rangers would encounter them. They’re a merc unit, but they’ll take contacts to just cause mayhem (sometimes paid for by the Dra’jn). They’re also involved in the black market, so if any Rangers are involved in any, shall we say “creative funding” operations, they may run into the Death Gan (either as buyers or competition). If a building or starship is blown up, and both the Dra’jn and Guavian Death Gang are in the area, it’s honestly a toss up which group could be responsible.
