# First Order Remnants

![][image24]  
Over 100 years ago, the Resistance and the Citizen's Fleet destroyed all Sith Order *Xyston-*class Star Destroyers over Exogol. The sinking of the Sith’s fleet of “planet-killer” star destroyers likely saved trillions of lives, and removed the greatest threat to peace from the galaxy. When word of Palpatine’s defeat got out, systems across the galaxy rose up and drove out the First Order. The First Order was driven back to their holdings in the Unknown Regions, where much like their spiritual ancestors, the Galactic Empire, the First Order fought a desperate but ultimately losing battle and fell to the Galactic Republic.

The systems of the former First Order were absorbed into the New Republic, and after several generations control of those systems has transferred from New Republic military governors to planetary-elected officials. Within those systems, it is still illegal to display any objects or symbols of the First Order, and public opinion of the Order is predominantly negative. For the most part the descendants of the First Order personnel are deeply ashamed of their heritage, and have taken steps to eradicate all traces of the First Order.

In spite of this, there are rumors that the First Order has survived. Records show that before their surrender, a few First Order warships fled deeper into the Unknown Regions. New Republic and Citizen's Fleet task forces hunted down most of the fleeing star destroyers, but a few ships are unaccounted for including two *Resurgent-*class Star Destroyers, the Annihilator and the Stormbringer*,* as well as a single *Matador IV* Siege Dreadnought, the Inevitable. In the intervening century, deep space explorers claim to have encountered these ships, usually in some hard-to-reach corner of the galaxy. Stories of the warships hiding in nebulae, asteroid fields, or other sensor-obscuring phenomena make frequent rounds in frontier cantinas. No one has been able to provide definitive proof these warships survived to the present day, and the “Remnant Order Fleet” is regarded as little more than myth and drunken spacer talk.
