# GenoHaradan

* An “open secret” among those in power across the galaxy (both legitimate and otherwise) is the GenHaradan.  They’ve been operating as the most effective assassins’ guild in the galaxy for *the last several centuries*. (Though it’s rumored that they’ve been operating for many millennia.)  
  * They are specters, ghosts, and an invisible hand. No one “contacts” the assassin’s guild.  When the need arises for such services, and inquiries are made through other channels (or sometimes... not made at all) the GenoHaradan just “know.”  
  * If they deem the target and contract worthy, they will reach out and offer their services.  
* They *are* the most effective assassins in operation.  Period.  It’s what they do.  It’s *all* they do.  
* The in-universe history of the GenHaradan is long (going back to 25,000 BBY), and they’ve morphed in goals and operational aspects throughout that time.  
  * But for the Fractured Republic Era, what the galaxy knows about them (those few who know of their existence) is what we’ve discussed so far.  
  * They are assassins-for-hire. The best. But the only way to hire them, is to make inquiries, and hope *they come to you*.  
* They have no political allegiance or affiliation (seemingly), and their services have been offered (sometimes nonsensically) to pauper planets dealing with a warlord, to syndicate leaders looking to exterminate a thorn in their side, and to military units waging wars and needing surgical strikes of enemy commanders.  
* In their actual operation (for the past few centuries), the GenoHaradan are radically decentralized. They have no base of operations, no facilities, no formal infrastructure. No trace of a trail to lead anyone to them.  
* They are controlled by 4 “Overseers” (like in ancient times), whose identities are unknown (even to each other).  
  * Communicating electronically with each other (infrequently), using baffled and encrypted channels \- the Overseers recruit, manage contracts and assignments, and identify potential targets.  
  * PLOT SEEDS:  One overseer is a member of Ranger High Command, one is a powerful industrialist in the FCC.  
* They’ve been functioning for the last several centuries as the most effective assassins’ guild in the galaxy  
  * But recently, the overseers have begun to put their sights higher (like the GenoHaradan of old) and realize they can use assassination to *control* aspects of the galaxy (think *Wanted*... “we make our own missions”)  
* **How to Use the GenoHaradan:  Carefully.  (LOL...)  This is a “non-existent” guild of the greatest assassins in the galaxy.**  
  * **Obviously, those investigating murder or assassination (such as the Rangers) may find their case is leading to a suspect that is part of the GenoHaradan**  
  * **But it’s also worth noting that *totally legitimate* Powers in the galaxy have (and likely regularly employ) the GenoHaradan.  For that matter... it’s probable that the Rangers have, as well.**  
  * **While other organized crime and syndicates may privately “chafe” at the GenoHaradan’s strangle-hold on the assassination business; they’re happy to take the “scraps” the guild chose not to offer a contract on.**  
    * **Plus... how are you going to be mad at the GenoHaradan? That’s not a wise strategy.**
