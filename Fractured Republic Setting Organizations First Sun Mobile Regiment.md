# First Sun Mobile Regiment

* Reaching back into Star Wars RPG history for this Fragments from the Rim, the First Sun Mobile Regiment is a long and storied military unit.   
  * They served the Galactic Empire until they were betrayed by a local Moff. Nearly wiped out, the First Sun slowly rebuilt during the New Republic. The regiment elected to stay out of the First Order/Resistance war, preferring to see which side the war ended on.   
  * In the aftermath, the First Sun have been busy, hired by any of the hundreds of independent systems as well as the triumvirates, commonwealths, federations, magistocracies, and principalities that have spung up (and fallen) in-between the galactic powers over the years.  
  * How to use the **First Sun Mobile Regiment**: the First Sun Mobile Regiment isn’t good or evil, just hired military might working for a paycheck.  
    * They might run afoul of the Rangers simply by being a potential opposition to the PC’s interests.  
      * They may be hired to attack a world the PCs are currently visiting.  
      * Might be hired protectors for a war criminal the PCs are trying to apprehend.
