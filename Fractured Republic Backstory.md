### Backstory

One hundred years ago, the galaxy was under the tyrannical grip of the Sith Eternal and the fanatical First Order. Now, a century after the Sith were defeated by Rey Skywalker and the Resistance, the galaxy lies fractured. Systems that were once members of the Republic declared independence, weary from years of oppression and neglect from corrupt foreign governments. Several of these independent systems banded together to form new alliances and governments. The unity enjoyed by the Old Republic is gone, replaced by a divided, fragmented galaxy.

Travel between galactic sectors has become difficult to navigate, both physically and politically. Many galactic powers established hyperspace interdiction zones at the edges of their borders. Distrust and suspicion prevail between the galactic powers, after years of watching neighboring systems cozying up to fascist regimes, corrupt rulers, and vile gangsters. Their arguments that they “did what was necessary to persevere and survive” fell on deaf ears for those who suffered under the Empire or the First Order. As a result, there are constant skirmishes and shadow wars between neighboring powers that threaten to flare up into open conflict.  

However, even in this precarious time there is hope. After the war against the First Order, the Resistance transformed into the Galactic Rangers \-- an independent organization dedicated to peace and justice. Like the Jedi Order of old, the Galactic Rangers dispatch agents, known as “Rangers”, throughout the galaxy to mediate disputes, intervene in conflicts, hunt down troublemakers, and support resistance movements against oppressive regimes. While many Jedi from the New Jedi Order have joined the Galactic Rangers, the majority of their ranks are made up of everyday people. These heroes have the will and drive to do what is right and necessary for galactic peace.

This is where your story begins. Whatever your background, intention, and motivation, you are a Ranger. You travel across the galaxy as needed, working to bring the light of hope to the desperate. Your arrival is often welcomed with open arms, occasionally with disdain, and even at times with open hostility. It’s not an easy job, but it’s a necessary one to help entice the galactic community to come together once more.
