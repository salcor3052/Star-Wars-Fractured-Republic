# Character Creation

![][image1]  
Fractured Republic utilizes all three lines of the *Star Wars Role-Playing Game* from EDGE Studio; *Edge of the Empire, Age of Rebellion,* and *Force and Destiny.* In addition, all books from the “universal” line are allowed (*Dawn of Rebellion, Rise of the Separatists, Collapse of the Republic,* etc.) Players are able to play a variety of heroes such as soldiers, bounty hunters, nobles, diplomats, scoundrels, technicians, pilots, and Force-wielders including Jedi. 

For most games, characters start as members of the Galactic Rangers, a peacekeeping organization recognized by most galactic powers. Players are free to determine why their character is in the Rangers and what their true intentions are.

Some play groups may want to play as more independent operatives, smugglers and fringers, or aligned with one of the galactic powers. Players and gamemasters are encouraged to have a Session Zero discussion of what themes, tones, and storylines they want to tell together.

# **Backgrounds**

**![][image2]**

Here are some possible background ideas for your character and where they come from. You are also encouraged to come up with additional backgrounds for your characters, but be sure to work with your gamemaster to ensure everyone is working in the same direciton of storytelling.

## **Citizen**

You come from a normal family from a normal world, and you wanted something beyond what the galaxy handed you. The Rangers represented a chance to see the galaxy and to put your talents to use helping other folks like yourself. Sometimes glamorous and often dangerous, you hold your own alongside your fellow Rangers to bring the light of hope to all.

## **Droid**

**![][image3]**

You are one of the galaxy’s many autonomous automatons. You could be fresh off the assembly line or have been around for centuries. Maybe you’re a production model with stock-standard parts, or you are a custom machine built for a specific purpose. You might even think you’re brand new but are actually much older than you seem thanks to a more recent memory wipe. No matter your origin, you are now with the Galactic Rangers \-- whether that’s as a Ranger or as another Ranger's companion (or property).

## **Former Military**

You served in one of the militaries of the various civilizations as a pilot, crew member, medic, soldier, mechanic, SpecOps, or any of a dozen possible military specializations. You might have been honorably (or dishonorably) discharged, or served your required minimum tour of duty. You might even have gone AWOL and are hiding in the Rangers, hoping not to be given any assignments in your former home galactic power.

## **Jedi**

**![][image4]**

You are a Jedi, one of the skilled Force-wielders trained at one of the New Jedi Order’s academies throughout the galaxy. The Galactic Rangers serve the greater good, and you wanted to be a part of that, or your master thought a stint with the Rangers would do you some good. You’re still a Jedi, but you are also a Ranger, able to bring the light of hope across the stars. The lack of direct supervision by your master at all times is a blessing and a curse. The Dark Side sometimes whispers swift and decisive answers to situations that would cause more problems than it would solve.

## **Law Enforcement**

Whether you were a security officer or a freelance bounty hunter, you came to the Rangers to bring criminals to justice. Peacekeeping appeals to you, but you wanted to do more than what your prior organization allowed. You may also simply enjoy the thrill of hunting down fugitives and collecting their bounties, and the Rangers have additional resources you did not have. In any case, after each successful mission you consider the galaxy to be a much safer place.

## **Outlaw**

**![][image5]**

You spent years on the opposite side of the law. Perhaps you did it because the government was oppressing your people, or out of desperation to survive, or simply because it was fun and thrilling to do so. Regardless, you’ve found your way to serving in the Rangers \-- either as penance for what you’ve done, to hide from prosecution, or as part of a long game to swindle something from someone. 

## **Spy**

Your facade is that of a normal Galactic Ranger, but your true loyalty lies with one of the galactic powers. The Corellian Alliance, Free Commerce Consortium, and even the Galactic Republic have agents within the Rangers, keeping an eye on the organization for signs of greater ambition. They also utilize the Rangers' galactic status to cross borders and gather intelligence on the other governments. Many Rangers would not respond favorably to the Rangers' good will being used in such a fashion, so you work to maintain your cover while answering to your handler back home.

## **Wanderer**

You travel where the galaxy takes you, and for now it’s brought you to the Rangers. You spent years drifting through the galaxy, from the gleaming spires of the many planetary ecumenopolises of the Core Worlds to the sparse settlements of the Outer Rim. At some point your path crossed that of the Rangers, and you decided to join up for a while. How long is still to be determined.

# **Motivations**

All characters should choose four motivations for their characters; Strength, Flaw, Desire, and Fear. These selections and their effect on your character use the Motivations rules from the *Genesys Role Playing Game.*

---

# **Obligation/Duty/Morality**

**![][image7]**

The three “role-playing mechanics” from the three Star Wars RPG lines are handled differently in **Fractured Republic.**

## **Starting Obligation**

All characters start with a 10-point Obligation, per the rules from *Edge of the Empire,* even if the character’s career is from *Age of Rebellion, Force and Destiny*, or one of the Universal rulebooks. Characters may not increase the rating of their Obligation in exchange for additional starting XP or credits, per the rules in *Edge of the Empire.*

## **Morality**

The Morality mechanic from *Force ad Destiny* is applied to all PCs with a Force Rating. Characters that acquire a Force Rating during play have the Morality mechanic applied to their character at that time. 

Force sensitive characters may not gain extra XP or Credits from affects to their starting Morality score. They may choose to start as Dark Side characters at the cost of 5 starting XP

## **Duty**

The Duty mechanic from *Age of Rebellion* is not used in this setting.

---

# **Starting XP**

Characters in **Fractured Republic** start with an extra 10 points of starting XP. This XP may be used to purchase skill ranks, talents, and Characteristic increases. 

Characters also start with another 10 points of earned XP. This XP may be used to purchase additional skill ranks and talents, Force powers, and Specialization trees. It may not be used to increase Characteristics.

Characters also start with one free Specialization tree in addition to the one they begin with. This is to help players realize their character concept. Players do not have to select this at game start, and may choose their free Specialization tree later on.

TL:DR

* \+10 xp for anything  
* \+10 xp for anything except Characteristics  
* 1 free Specialization (Career or Non-Career, can be "banked" and claimed later in play)

All characters receive an extra 1,500 credits to purchase equipment.

# House Rules

# House Rules

# **Force Use**

**![][image9]**

When rolling for Force Points as part of a skill check, you may always spend a Destiny Point to use Dark Side points generated by the Force Dice (or to use Light Side points if you’re a Dark Side character), even if you have already used a Destiny Point during your turn. Spending a Destiny Point in this manner does not prevent you from spending another Destiny Point later during your turn. In each instance, a Light Side Destiny Point must still be available to spend.

---

# **Vehicle Combat**

**![][image10]**

Any encounters involving vehicles use the Vehicle Combat rules as presented in the *Genesys Role-Playing Game*.

---

# **Morality and Conflict**

**![][image11]**

Conflict points are accumulated as per the rules in *Force and Destiny.* The Conflict roll may not happen at the end of each session. Instead, the gamemaster determines when the roll occurs, usually at the end of story beats and milestones. This may mean that Conflict accumulates over the course of two or three sessions, depending on the length of a session.
