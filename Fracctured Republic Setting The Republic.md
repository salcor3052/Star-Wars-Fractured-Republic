# Galactic Republic

![][image12]

The once galaxy-spanning Republic has diminished in size over this past century, but still remains a prominent, and potent, galactic power. Its territory includes many of the Core Worlds, Expansion region, and the Colonies region, but also extends into several isolated clusters of systems in the Outer Rim Territories. This has created some difficulties for the Republic Senate to govern and the New Republic Defense Force to defend.

The New Republic strives to return to its former glory, but would prefer to accomplish that through treaties and alliances rather than conquest. Of all the new political powers, the New Republic remains the most open and welcoming, its borders lack the Hyperspace Interdiction Zones that permeate other galactic states. The perception that the Republic has nothing to hide does help with galactic relations. This makes it somewhat easier to smuggle goods into and out of the Republic, and keeps the New Republic Armed Forces busy. 

Of all the galactic powers, the New Republic is the most spread out. All the other major civilizations in the galaxy hail from worlds that were once part of the Republic (or the Empire), and thus some wholly reside within the larger border of the New Republic. This strains the New Republics supply and logistics lines, and forces the New Republic Armed Forces to establish local bases and strongholds throughout its territory. This has also caused some tension with neighboring states, but it is a necessary price to pay to support the New Republic’s expansive territory.
