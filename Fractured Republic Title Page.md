# Title Page

**A GALAXY DIVIDED. One hundred years since the defeat of the SITH ORDER, the galaxy lies fragmented. The NEW REPUBLIC seeks reunification, but systems that suffered under the GALACTIC EMPIRE and the FIRST ORDER are reluctant to once again rely on distant governments for safety. New galactic powers rise, and with them isolationism and suspicion.**

**Among the nefarious opportunists seeking to take advantage of this discord is DRA'JN, a ruthless terrorist organization bent on galactic chaos. Their actions have disrupted planetary economies, bolstered oppressive despots, and brought suffering to billions across the galaxy.**

**While the NEW JEDI ORDER continues to grow, their numbers are too small to stem the flow of galactic tensions. The mantle of intergalactic peacekeepers has been picked up by the GALACTIC RANGERS, an army of soldiers, scientists, ambassadors, and citizens devoted to oppose the growing darkness in the galaxy…**
