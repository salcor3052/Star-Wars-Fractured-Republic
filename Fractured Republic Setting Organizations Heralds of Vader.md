# Heralds of Vader

* What we need here is a good-old-fashioned TRUE CULT.  Not a part of of larger organization \- but a real CULT.  Galaxy-spanning, and creepy, and dangerous.  
* What’s more \- we need a Sith cult  
  * A cult who believes in the power of the Sith that was repressed and squashed, and want to bring it back into the light.  
* Per their own (likely falsified) lore, they began by devotees to Lord Vader from his throne planet of Mustafar, over a century ago.  
  * (In reality, the Heralds of Vader started about 20 years ago).  
* The Heralds of Vader is, well and truly, a CULT.  Their devotees are literally worshiping The Dark Side of the Force.  But... they’re also \- essentially \- a social club.  
  * Their members are mostly nobles from across the galaxy, meaning that the cult operates as basically an “open secret” in noble circles (almost like Skull & Bones).  
  * For most, joining it is more of a means to conspiratorial power among the wealthy elites than anything else  
* But there ARE force sensitives in their ranks, learning rather dark shit  
* SECRET:  They have possession of an Ancient Sith Holocron (damaged)  
  * The entity within is guiding them \- but they really don’t know how to use it fully  
* **How to Use the Heralds of Vader:  They’re a very well organized street gang with a nasty reputation who likes pissing off the Hutts.  A lot to work with here, as both antagonists and allies.**
