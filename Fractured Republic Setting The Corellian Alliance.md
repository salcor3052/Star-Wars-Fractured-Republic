# Corellian Alliance

![][image15]  
One of the first political states to separate from the New Republic, the Corellian Alliance has emerged as a formidable power and an exemplar for other states and unions that wish to remain apart from the Galactic Republic. After the Corellian Uprising against the First Order, a group of outspoken activists and officials \-- calling themselves the “Sovereign” party \-- called for Corellia to be free from the New Republic, citing the larger galactic power’s inability to govern effectively or protect its citizens. When the New Republic moved their capital to Chandrila instead of Corellia, many Corellians saw that as the final straw. The Sovereigns won their vote for Corellia to secede from the New Republic.

Within two months, Corellia formed alliances with the other inhabited worlds within its star system. Selonia, Talus, and Tralus signed the Corellia System Treaty, proclaiming Corellia as the capital and the entire Corellian system as its domain. Within a decade, other nearby worlds were courted and elected to join the Corellian Alliance, the new name for the small but powerful galactic state.

Thanks to the shipyards on Corellia, Nubia, and Duro, the Alliance maintains one of the largest and most powerful navies in the galaxy. Corellian Engineering Corporation still sells yachts, freighters, transports, and even warships to other galactic powers, but retains the more advanced ships for the Alliance Navy.
