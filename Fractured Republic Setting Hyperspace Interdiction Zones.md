# Hyperspace Interdiction Zones 

Hyperspace Interdiction Zones are regions along hyperspace routes that are blockaded and patrolled by various galactic powers, usually at the edge of that power's stellar border. For the most part, most borders remain open to galactic travel. However, an Interdiction Zone can be established along the borders at a moments notice. Large space stations are posted along the hyperspace route and utilize gravity well projectors to create a massive gravity shadow into hyperspace, blocking the route to all traffic. Any ship traveling that route in hyperspace is immediately pulled into realspace and into the middle of the interdiction fleet. Ships are then processed and inspected under the watchful guns of the interdiction fleet before they are either turned away or allowed to continue along their journey.

Interdiction stations have a dedicated fleet defending them, strong enough to hold against all but the largest flotillas. The ships assigned to interdiction fleets are usually specialized in defense, with heavy shields and tractor beam projectors. Their job is to hold any attackers or blockade runners fast, and send an alert to a nearby system for the heavier attack ships to assist, if needed.The Galactic Republic and Corellian Alliance maintain several interdiction stations along the Corellian Run, Corellian Trade Spine, Hydian Way, and the Perlemian Trade Route. These Interdiction Zones are active only at certain times, when mandated by their governments. The Free Commerce Consortium operates interdiction zones along every hyperspace route through their territory, charging a nominal fee for all traffic as well as varying tariffs on all good transported through their sectors. The SenJu Confederation also maintains active interdiction zones at all hyperspace route border crossings, partially to keep smugglers and foreign dignitaries out but also keeping any Confederate citizens from leaving. The Mandalorian Protectorate and the Hutt Cartels do not operate interdiction zones, citing such zones as fostering suspicion between powers and hindering galactic good will.
