# Galactic Rangers

![][image29]

## ***“Ignite the spark and light the fire of hope\!”***

**THE GALACTIC RANGERS**, sometimes simply referred to as “The Rangers”, are an independent, philanthropic, paramilitary organization. At the end of the First Order-Resistance War, the Resistance found itself in an unusual situation; with the defeat of the Sith Order, their reason for existing was gone. The Resistance no longer had a fascist, galaxy-spanning regime to “resist”. The surviving leadership considered disbanding the organization, but before such plans could be put into motion the group found a new purpose.

The political fallout from the war led to a fracturing of the [Galactic Republic](https://app.legendkeeper.com/a/worlds/clmywn5qlc9u70842gmlc344a/clmyzyw4s005w033cfihqcnrz) and the rise of several new galactic powers. Many politicians acknowledged that negotiations between the new governments would be difficult, there was an air of distrust between planets that fell in with the First Order and those that attempted to resist. Moreover, the galaxy lacked an authority that could serve as peacekeepers, calming tensions and pursuing criminals across these new borders. With the [Galactic Republic](https://app.legendkeeper.com/a/worlds/clmywn5qlc9u70842gmlc344a/clmyzyw4s005w033cfihqcnrz) military a shadow of its former self, and the Jedi Order diminished to a single individual, the galaxy needed an organization that could provide aid and assistance unilaterally on a galactic scale.

After a lengthy discussion between Generals Dameron, Finn, and Calrissian, as well as Jedi Master Rey Skywalker, the Resistance would fulfill that needed role. The Resistance transformed into a group that “would bring the light of hope to the galaxy, and show what we can do togehter, as one\!" To help foster their new role, they took up the mantle of the Old Republic paramilitary group that supported the Jedi Order's efforts, the Galactic Rangers. The leaders of the new Galactic Rangers traveled to each of the galactic powers, petitioning them for support and the authority to provide aid whenever and wherever needed. After several years of negotiations and political maneuvering, the Galactic Rangers are the first galaxy-recognized peacekeeping organization since the fall of the Jedi Order. 

![][image30]

The Galactic Rangers have hundreds of thousands of members spread to all corners of the known galaxy (and even into the Unknown Regions). Initially comprised of former soldiers and law enforcement personnel, their roster now includes thousands of civilian doctors, technicians, administrators, negotiators, and social caregivers. A Ranger's duties could take them across the galaxy, but in practice most Rangers operate within an assigned sector. They operate as a combination of frontier marshal and freelance negotiator, providing aid to disaster areas, curtailing piracy and slavery, and mediating disputes.

The Galactic Rangers are not entirely “galaxy-recognized”, as there are a few prominent powers that refuse to acknowledge the Rangers' authority. Rangers are recognized by the Republic as “non-aligned peacekeepers”, and most worlds in the Republic favor actions taken by the Rangers as long as they are just. The find similar recognition throughout the [Mandalorian Protectorate](https://app.legendkeeper.com/a/worlds/clmywn5qlc9u70842gmlc344a/clmyzzcw90068033c7ik3x7jt), but are often escorted by members of the [Mandalorian Protectors](https://app.legendkeeper.com/a/worlds/clmywn5qlc9u70842gmlc344a/clvpirth40az8356jm98zudyx) while within their territory. In the [Corellian Alliance](https://app.legendkeeper.com/a/worlds/clmywn5qlc9u70842gmlc344a/clmyzz55n0062033cjhihw3sz) and the [Free Commerce Consortium](https://app.legendkeeper.com/a/worlds/clmywn5qlc9u70842gmlc344a/clmyzzkfu006e033cvyg7aoyj), the Rangers are given polite courtesy in the pursuit of criminals or when participating in disaster relief, but are generally kept at arms length by those governments and security forces. In Hutt Space, the Galactic Rangers are seen as nuisances and are frequently “encouraged” to leave (or at least to mind their own business). Rangers are openly regarded as troublemakers and provocateurs in the [SenJu Confederation](https://app.legendkeeper.com/a/worlds/clmywn5qlc9u70842gmlc344a/clmyzzrpv006k033ctra5tq88), and most Rangers need to go undercover to operate there.

Despite of the challenges they face, their limited funding, and the nearly insurmountable task of maintaining galactic peace, the Galactic Rangers continue to protect and serve the galaxy, striving for the safety of all beings.

# Ranger Operations

## Structure

The Rangers are an expansive organization. They are responsible for patrolling most of the known galaxy, keeping the peace and assisting in disaster relief. Such tasks were daunting for the fully equipped militaries throughout history such as the Grand Army of the Republic, Galactic Empire, and the First Order. For the often understaffed and underfunded Rangers, the task appears impossible. Despite this herculean task, the Rangers’ tenacity and determination motivates them to frequently accomplish the impossible.

Rangers are organized into squads. Each squad typically consists of rangers from a variety of skills and backgrounds to be able to adapt to any situation they encounter. In practice, squads tend to develop specialties for certain mission types, depending on the skill set of the individual unit members. While Ranger Command would prefer to see each team diverse and capable enough to tackle anything thrown at them, they recognize that it is best for mission success and morale to assign missions to squads with an aligned expertise. Given the size and scope of Ranger operations through the galaxy, missions often must be assigned to the closest squad available. Each Ranger squad is lead by a Lead Ranger, or “LR”. While most squads have the LR appointed by the Rangers, several squads have elected their own LR, or swap who is the LR based on the needs of the mission.

Each squad reports to a Ranger Captain who coordinates and directs the efforts of several squads — typically three to six squads. Ranger Captains frequently command their own squad and go on missions with them. The Ranger Captain reports to a Ranger Commander who is usually in charge of an outpost or starship. Commanders are frequently on-scene for larger Ranger missions such as mass-casualty disasters, large military engagements, or other fleet operations. Ranger Brigadiers and Vice Admirals are the highest “field officers” in the Rangers. Brigadiers and Vice Admirals are usually called in for prominent negotiations, mediations, or when the Rangers need to send a diplomatic envoy to a state function. The highest ranks within the Rangers are the Generals and Admirals of the Ranger High Command. They manage the day-to-day operations of the Galactic Rangers and advise the Ranger Aurek.

Appointed by High Command, Ranger Aurek is the overall commander of the Galactic Rangers. They are the face of the Rangers, making appearances and giving speeches when called on, but also taking command in the field for major operations. In the tradition established by General Poe Dameron and Finn, the Ranger Aurek is frequently a Ranger who is still combat-active and capable of holding their own in personal, tactical, and strategic combat. There have been brief periods where there were no candidates for the Ranger Aurek, and the overall efforts of the Rangers were directed by committe in the High Command. This only lasts as long as it takes to find a new Ranger Aurek candidate and appoint them to the role.
