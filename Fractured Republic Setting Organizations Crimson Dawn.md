# Crimson Dawn

The criminal organization introduced to us in *Solo*, and expanded upon in the Star Wars comics. It fell into ruin and was a fading memory by the time of the First Order. In the years since then, a new face and a new power has brought the Crimson Dawn back into the galactic scene.

* Reborn under the direction of a woman named Leesai Aldus  
  * She is a descendant of Cadeliah, the orphan who was raised by Qi’ra, the last great leader of Crimson Dawn.  
    * She has taken the fortune left to her ancestor and used it to build an influential criminal empire with a network of spies and informants through the galaxy.  
  * As the Hutts consolidated their Union, Crimson Dawn operated elsewhere in the Outer Rim and unaligned systems.  
    * It is believed that Crimson Dawn is as large a criminal enterprise as the Hutt Cartels, but this may simply be baseless boasting and speculation.  
  * As is tradition, all members have the Crimson Dawn tattoo on them somewhere easily accessible (although not always showing).  
  * How to Use the Crimson Dawn: when you need a crime syndicate with creepy force-sensitives in the mix...  
    * Crimson Dawn’s operations have crystalized to focus heavily (some would say... too heavily...) on what the Ghosts of Maul can provide.   
    * Like any syndicate, they engage in racketeering and smuggling; but they’ve taken a premier spot within the criminal ranks as enforcers and bodyguards for hire.  
      * This also extends into espionage and assassination of high-risk targets.  
    * Crimson Dawn’s natural enemy in the crime world, however \- is Black Sun. Competing for the same operations and targets, the two syndicates are continually at odds.  
      * Overtures of alliance (or at least a firm peace) extended by the Dawn to Black Sun leadership have reportedly gone unanswered.  
      * It’s even rumored that Crimson Dawn has attempted to assassinate Black Sun leadership at certain points \- which has (as of yet) been not only unsuccessful, but *wildly* unsuccessful.  
    * When they run afoul of the Rangers \- it’s for reasons you’d expect (crime and whatnot); but the frequent use of force-sensitive “thugs” (the Ghosts of Maul) make fighting them exceptionally dangerous.  
      * The Rangers are rumored to have assets inside Crimson Dawn, or at the least, provide regular counter-intelligence \- all to stoke flames to keep the organization in a state of war or distrust with Black Sun.
