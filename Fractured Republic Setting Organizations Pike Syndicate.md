# Pike Syndicate

* Operating from their homeworld of Oba Diah for millenia, the Pyke Syndicate (or Pyke Family) has for centuries had as overpowering grip on the spice trade in the galaxy \- once as major traffickers in the Spice Cartel \- until eventually gaining complete control over the Spice Mines of Kessel; ultimately gaining near sole ownership of spice production runs between Kessel and the core.  
  * The Pykes (it’s worth noting) are a species \- the Pyke species \- and YES, through galactic history, their Syndicates has been limited exclusively to Pykes.  
* They have a long and interesting history of fighting with and working with varied factions and syndicates; aiding the Sith, the Jedi, and powers big and small and inbetween for various reasons \- as they ARE a minor military power in their own right, with fleets, enforcers, and trained soldiers.  
  * Mostly \- to PROTECT their massive spice shipments.  
* In the era of the fractured republic, they are still “the” major producer of raw spice (and, ultimately, the defacto “owners” of the Spice Trades), with even more power than before \- as the fractured republic has given them more avenues and opportunities.  
  * Their transport and production of spice also leads to related avenues of skulduggery; such as smuggling (since they’re pretty good at it), transport protection, and slavery (since they almost exclusively use slaves in the mines on Kessel).  
* **How to Use the Pyke Syndicate:  They’re the powerful drug cartel, ultimately \- with all that this entails from a story standpoint.**  
  * **They are highly insular, and interested in maintaining their stranglehold on spice production in the galaxy \- whatever that takes.**  
    * **They do NOT react well to competition in this area, and respond with extreme force**  
  * **For Rangers and others who may encounter their nefarious activities, there’s a lot to work with beyond just combatting spice dealers:**  
    * **Abduction of refugees (to be used as slaves) is a booming business in the fractured republic**
