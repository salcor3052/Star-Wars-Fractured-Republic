# Black Sun

* Still a major crime syndicate, they don’t possess the reach or power they once did (following the death of Prince Xixor).  At one point, they even operated as a “loose conglomeration of families” (mafioso style) following Xixor’s death, with the varied leaders controlling their own affairs and secretly (sometimes openly) competing with each other for territory and operational control \- while all still enjoying the traipses of being called “Black Sun.”  
  * Much of this changed about 3 decades ago \- as a seemingly new figure took the reigns as the new “head” of Black Sun.  
* This enigmatic leader \- this new “head” of Black Sun \- re-established the old controls and leadership caste system within Black Sun, installing a cadre of 7 “Vigos” (the Bocce word for “uncle”) to oversee the syndicate’s galactic operations.  
  * In effect, these Vigos are the most powerful people in the syndicate, but are unwaveringly loyal to their leader.  
* This new enigmatic leader is rarely seen in public, and there are those (even in the syndicate) who believe she is a myth. But those few who have seen her note her as a very cold, very beautiful human woman with blonde hair \- who doesn’t seem to age.  
  * With a strangely encyclopedic knowledge of Black Sun’s operations and resources, she rose to power quickly through alliances, manipulation, and outright assassination \- eventually becoming the recognized “head” of the syndicate \- and installing her own people as Vigos in the organization.  
    * PLOT SEED: The 7 Vigos are all HRDs (or ARDs \- Alien Replica Droids); people Guri could trust  
    * PLOT SEED: Unbeknownst to everyone, Guri and the Vigos are the real power and funding behind the “droid underground” in the galaxy  
* **How to Use Black Sun:** As a classic crime syndicate with a creepily competent leadership \- very smart, very calculated  
  * Today, Black Sun’s enterprises are varied; but remain heavily focused on smuggling, racketeering, blackmail, and espionage.  
    * As mentioned, this regularly puts them at odds with Crimson Dawn.  
  * More than most syndicates, though, Black Sun is trying hard to crawl back to prominence through good-old-fashioned espionage and blackmail. Politicians, corporate leaders, military leaders, and those in power throughout the galaxy... Black Sun’s focus is to control them.  
    * This means getting dirt on them; incriminating evidence that can be used to form a tight leash.  
    * All this means that they have a shocking amount of control (even in their “still rebuilding” state) over legislative activities and courts of law.  
  * As far as encountering the Rangers, it’s a crime syndicate who will run afoul of them.  
    * But operations with Black Sun tend to be “higher class” crime when the rangers are brought in.  (E.G.: Corporate espionage, organized spying, and sedition)  
    * Black Sun almost always has an “out,” though \- their operations are planned with precision, and it’s not uncommon (despite Ranger efforts) to bring Black Sun enforcers or operators to trial, only to find charges mysteriously dismissed due to issues with evidence chain custody, or witnesses mysteriously changing testimonies.  
  * PLOT SEED:  For a few in Ranger Command, Black Sun is the “Moby Dick” to be brought down... somehow…
