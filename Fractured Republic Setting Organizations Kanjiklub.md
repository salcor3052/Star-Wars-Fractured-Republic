# Kanjiklub

* Also known as the Kanji Gang \- historically has operated throughout the Outer Rim. The Kanji’s history is marked by rebellion against the Hutts (as the original Kanjis were renegade slaves of the Hutts).  
* Known for their iconic use of makeshift weaponry (a holdover and cultural badge of honor from their time as renegade slaves), they’ve continued to stay consistent well into the era of the Fractured Republic as feared raiders and a nasty (if well organized) inter-galactic street gang.  
* In our era, they specialize in extortion, ransom, and larceny (both large and small); and operate heavily in the outskirts of the Nal Niboba Union (for no other reason than to stick it to the Hutts)  
  * Kanjiklub still (a century later) hold the Hutts as blood enemies; and their recruitment continues to be from sentient beings that have been enslaved or wronged by the Hutts  
* Recently, however \- Kanjiklub has expanded their operations to the spice trade \- making major waves.  
  * They’ve been producing and selling (exclusively) a galaxy-class spice called “Nebula Blue”  
  * It’s creator is an enigmatic person known only as “Hayzen” whom Kanjiklub protects with fervor (It’s rumored that Hayzen is a former core worlds commoner who developed a “perfect formula”)  
  * As a result of this \- Kanjiklub has seriously run afoul of the Pykes   
* How to Use Kanjiklub:  They’re a very well organized street gang with a nasty reputation who likes pissing off the Hutts.  A lot to work with here, as both antagonists and allies.  
  * For those looking to smuggle objects, people, or information in or out of the Nal Niboba Union \- there are few better at it than Kanjiklub (who would probably be more receptive, as well, as long as they were sticking it to the NNU)  
  * Nebula Blue can be a major campaign in its own right \- just discovering the manufacturer
