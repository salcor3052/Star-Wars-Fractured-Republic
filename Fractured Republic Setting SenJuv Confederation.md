# SenJu Confederation

![][image21]  
A once-unified region, the Senex-Juvex Confederation is now a hot-bed of intrigue and infighting among the member worlds. Originally the sector was controlled by a council of planetary oligarchs. The “Council of Lords” maintained a peaceful relationship with the old Republic, and paid tribute to the Empire. The Council of Lords sided with the First Order, but suffered from a massive uprising when the fascist Final Order was defeated.

The fall of the Sith Order at the hands of the Resistance and the Citizens' Navy inspired the working classes within the sector to rise up against the ruling houses. While some of these rebellions were brutally struck down, many were successful in overthrowing the ruling noble houses of their worlds. Eventually, the newly-liberated worlds of the sector banded together in a loose alliance, calling the new power the Senex-Juvex Confederation. Sadly, several Confederation worlds are now under control of charismatic yet corrupt authoritarians, and suffer from greater economic divides than under the rule of the oligarchs. There are a few worlds that were successful in establishing true democratic, or at least functional, governments that act on behalf of the masses.

Life in the Confederacy can be brutal. The autocratic rulers in control of the Confederation worlds demand fealty from their citizenry, and utilize their militaries to enforce their rule. Few beings can ever afford to leave the Sector, lacking the funds or contacts to emigrate to other systems. 

Reports are that the civilian population of several Confederate systems are attempting to organize and overthrow their corrupt leaders. Time will tell if these uprisings are successful, or if they are stomped down before they can even occur.
