# Sith Dominion

![][image26]  
The **Sith Dominion** are the descendants of Pureblood Sith expatriates who left the Sith Empire at the end of the Great Galactic War. After centuries of oppression and injustices from within the Empire by non-Sith, “The Exiles” fled to the **Unknown Regions** with their loyal supporters. There they began to rebuild their society, a mirror of the caste system that was in-place before the coming of the Sith Order. The Exiles declared their empire the Sith Dominion, a totalitarian regime built on slavery and oppression strengthened by total devotion to the Dark Side. 

For thousands of years, they watched the rise and fall of the Sith Order, culminating in their defeat on Exegol. With the extinction of the “false” Sith Lords, the Dominion set their plans for return in motion. They slowly migrated back to one of their former holdings \-- Ziost. Determined to keep the galaxy from prematurely learning of the Sith Dominion, the resettlement of Ziost was slow and cautious. Convoy ships traveled in small numbers through non-traditional hyperspace lanes to avoid detection. When convoy ships encountered anyone from outside the Dominion, they were swiftly destroyed by the Sith. Once the planet’s base infrastructure was sufficiently restored, the bulk of the Sith Dominion’s populace were moved to Ziost through use of ships equipped with Force-Drives \-- an ancient method of faster-than-light travel that is powered by the Dark Side of the Force. 

During their repatriation of Ziost, the Sith Dominion sent agents throughout the galaxy to wage a secret information war campaign. These Darkseekers swept across the galaxy, infiltrating galactic cartography hubs, libraries, and information centers. The Shadow Blades proceeded to delete Ziost from every record they could find. The operation took more than twenty years to complete, even then there was no possible way the Shadow Blades could alter the records of every data archive. The Sith Dominion elected to wait another generation before beginning to act against the galaxy, giving time for older ships to update their stellar charts with the new altered data, or letting old archives fall further into obscurity.

One hundred years after the fall of the Sith Order, the Sith Dominion finally set their plan into motion.

# **The Plan**

The Dominion are determined to enact vengeance on the galaxy that has slighted their people for thousands of years. The Sith’ari declared that the Dominion would succeed where the false-lords of the Sith Order failed. They have several objectives that the Dominion is working to complete.

**Destruction of the Republic**

The Galactic Republic represents a sizable threat to the Dominion’s plans. In the time since the Final Order’s defeat on Exogol, the Republic’s efforts to re-establish their governing body have met with resistance. Many former Republic worlds banded together to forge new galactic powers. This division of power makes the Republic vulnerable, but they are still a threat. The Republic Navy maintains the largest fleet of warships of all the galactic powers, and their military industrial base is the largest in the galaxy. Still, their armies haven’t been tested in any sizable conflicts for several generations, and should fall before the battle-hardened might of the Dominion Legions.

Once the Dominion reveals themselves to the galaxy, they will strike at the Republic on multiple fronts, keeping them off-balance and targeting the New Republic’s ability to wage war. Once the New Republic’s military infrastructure is in shambles, the Dominion then plans to attack population centers, attacks that devastate populations killing billions until the New Republic surrenders and bends knee to the Sith Dominion.

**Subjugation of the Hutt Cartels**

The powerful Hutt kajidics have ruled Hutt Space for thousands of years, in a manner that is ironically similar to the manner of the Dominion. Through slavery and rigid hierarchy, the Hutts have grown in power and wealth. Aside from the New Republic, the Hutt Kajidics are a potent force if they were to band together against a common foe. 

Initially, the Sith Dominion plans to send diplomats and ambassadors to the kajidics in order to establish peaceful relationships with the Dominion. Behind the scenes, the Dominion plans to use back-room deals and Shadow Blades to ferment in-fighting between the hutt clans, intent on starting a civil war to weaken and destabilize the cartels. Once they are sufficiently weakened, the Dominion would move in and assume control of the cartels, either directly or by proxy with other criminal organizations.

**Expansion of the Sith Dominion**

The ultimate goal of the Dominion is the establishment of a new galactic-wide empire. The Sith’ari is patient in the pursuit of this goal, as they utilize Sith sorcery and alchemy to extend their lifespan. They are content to be methodical and precise with their conquest, striking when opportunity arises and consolidating their holdings to prevent any rebellions from  fermenting.

**Eradication of the Jedi Order**

Considered to be the least pressing obstacle, the resurgent New Jedi Order is a shadow of its pre-Empire glory. The Dominion does not consider the Jedi Order a serious threat, merely a pestilence that must be stamped out when it is encountered. Shadow Blades have taken note of the locations of several Jedi Temples, many of which are to be targeted for infiltration and assassination of the Knights and Masters, or otherwise obliterated from orbit by starship bombardment.

# Culture

![][image27]  
The Sith Dominion is built on a society of oppression, meritocracy, and absolute rule. To rise up from the caste you were born to is an arduous journey, while it is easier for one to fall from grace to a lower caste. 

The Dominion caste system is divided into six tiers. Most subjects that are born to a tier either remain there their entire lives or fall to a lower tier. 

* **Archassai**: The highest tier of castes and the ruling class, the Archessai include the noble Pureblood Sith families that led the Exiles for millenia. Each member of the Archassai are powerful dark side users, many with powers that rival the most legendary and infamous Sith Lords. The Sith’ari is the leader of the Archassai, and the Dominion at large.  
* **Kissai**: The priest class of the Sith Dominion and the old Sith Empire. The Kissai are masterful alchemists and dark side practitioners. They study the nature of the Dark Side, and employ that knowledge in forging Dark-Side infused objects for the Dominion. Kissai are found in every community within the Dominion, serving as the voice of the Sith’ari in that area.  
* **Massassi**: The warrior caste of the Sith Dominion, most Massassi are large, hulking brutes compared to other Pureblood Sith. Massasi are the bulk of the Dominion’s armed forces, equipped with the best arms and armor. In the Golden Age of the Sith, Massassi were only slightly better than mindless brutes. Through the centuries, Massassi have evolved into intelligent, cunning warriors, able to manage the affairs of their houses and caste. Socially they are the third highest caste.   
  Since the Exile, several members of lower castes earned a place in the Massassi caste. These smaller warriors frequently pilot the Dominion’s vehicle assets, but on occasion they can be found shoulder to shoulder with their larger caste-mates. There is little gruff or belligerent attitudes from “true Massassi” against their smaller brethren (beyond what is typical among the caste). As long as they kill the Dominion’s enemy, it doesn’t matter the warrior’s size.  
* **Zuguruk**: A caste of makers and engineers, the Zuguruk caste include the numerous beings required to build and maintain the Dominion’s technological assets. The Zuguruk are responsible for managing the Infinite Engine, and production of the Dominion’s military assets. All civilian construction projects are under the purview of the Zuguruak family houses as well. Socially, members of the Zuguruk houses enjoy a higher standard of living than the laborers and the slaves, and are in a constant struggle to gain favor with their Kissai and Archassai leaders.  
* **Dotrazek**: The most populous of the Sith Dominion castes, Dotrazek are the laborers of their society. They are arguably the most important caste in the Dominion. The Archassai need Dotrazek to rule, the Kissai and Zuguruk need Dotrazek to worship and build their great empire, and the slaves need to hope for the possibility of rising out of their servitude. If the Dotrazek ever decided to use their numbers to influence the Dominion, they might have a chance of success. Unfortunately for them, the Archassai and Kissai have done a masterful job of manipulation within the Dominion, brutally eradicating any spark of defiance and rebellion among the populace.  
* **The Chained**: The lowest rung of the Dominion's caste system, the Chained are worked to exhaustion and often death by their masters. They handle the worst and most dangerous tasks, such as mining, toiling in the fields, and manual labor in harsh environments. Occasionally, individuals among the Chained are rewarded for years of dutiful service with an elevation to the Dotrazek caste. This is a lie, for while Pureblood Sith slaves are allowed to join the Dotrazek, any non-Sith who are “rewarded” are slain once they are out of view of the other slaves. This faint hope for something better is what motivates the Chained to slave away at their endless, monotonous, and hazardous tasks.

## **Caste Elevation**

While the Dominion rely on oppression and tyranny to keep their population in check, they have learned that it is more advantageous to nurture in-fighting among their subjects. They do this through programs that look for promising candidates to advance to a higher caste. The challenges these candidates face are daunting, and often lethal, but the hope of bettering oneself within the Dominion is enough to keep most focused on their individual desires instead of more widespread social reform. The Dominion even subtly encourages candidates to sabotage the efforts of their fellow competitors, continuing to foster in-fighting among the castes instead of to the Sith Lords themselves.

# Darkseekers

The Darkseekers are the hunters of the Sith Dominion. With their red lightsaber blades, they hunt down and eradicate threats to the Dominion.

# Infinite Engine

One of the great powers employed by the Sith Dominion is the **Star Forge**. The original Star Forge was constructed by the Rakkata Star Empire over 25,000 years ago. It was destroyed during the Old Republic by Darth Revan and Bastilla Shan, but a piece of the Star Forge was recovered and given to a group of Ongree who ended up on Nar Shadda. There the Infinite Engine grew, providing protection and sustenance to the Ongree enclave. Tragedy would eventually befall the enclave as the Sith Darkseekers found the enclave, slaughtered the entire enclave population, and left with the Infinite engine.

Thousands of years later, the Infinite Engine has grown to a size rivaling that of the Galactic Empire’s Death Stars. The Infinite Engine works similarly to its predecessor, the Star Forge. The Engine draws raw materials from a star, utilizing those elements to construct near limitless resources \-- weapons, droids, warships, and other materials. The Dominion’s Zuguruk caste manages the Infinite Engine while the Kissai priests utilize powerful sith alchemy and magic to infuse certain constructs with great power. The result is a near limitless source of war materiel for the Dominion’s plot to subjugate the galaxy.
