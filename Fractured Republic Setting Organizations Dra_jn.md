# Dra'jn

![][image33]

Many want-to-be despots and bandit kings rose out of the chaos of the [Fractured Republic](https://app.legendkeeper.com/a/worlds/clmywn5qlc9u70842gmlc344a/clmywn6wt0002033cm6udwdil) era. The most successful, and dangerous, is Dra’jn \-- a mysterious terrorist organization responsible for acts of chaos and destruction across the galaxy. The earliest confirmed act by Dra’jn occurred fifty years ago, and they have sown acts of terror across the galaxy ever since.

Originally believed to be mere mercenaries and assassins for hire, Dra’jn’s motivations go deeper than they first appeared. Their ultimate goal is the accumulation of power. Among the criminal underworld and intelligence agencies, Dra’jn is regarded as an army-for-hire, willing to wage war for anyone with credits.  Among their ranks are downtrodden outcasts, aimless mercenaries, and nihilistic fanatics. Dra’jn’s reach extends to the far corners of the galaxy, practically every major population center has a cell of Dra’jn agents waiting to cause terror and mayhem in the advancement of their cause.

Dra’jn is led by an enigmatic, masked figure known as “[The Discordant](https://app.legendkeeper.com/a/worlds/clmywn5qlc9u70842gmlc344a/clmz0w0fi000q356mktbgf04x)”. Clad in a faceless mask, adorned with deep red robes and scaled armor, the Discordant wields the charisma and authority necessary to lead their fanatical soldiers. No one has ever seen beneath the Discordant's mask, and no clear sign of age, gender, or species can be accurately determined. It’s possible that the current Discordant is the same being that led Dra’jn when they first appeared on the galactic scene, or it’s possible that the Discordant has been portrayed by many since then. In either case, the cult-of-personality the Discordant wields has not diminished over the decades.

The galactic intelligence community questions where Dra’jn’s support comes from. The galaxy has no shortage of vile beings eager for a nefarious cause or ill-gotten credits, but the Dra’jn are equipped well-beyond any mere gang. Captured Dra’jn’s equipment have been examined by various intelligence organizations but none have been able to find any identifying markers as to who’s producing Dra'jn's arms and equipment. This points to an outside power as the manufacturer for the Dra’jn; an unknown entity with mysterious motivations that could spell doom for the galaxy.

### Dra’jn’s Secret Origins

Dra’jn first appeared in the galaxy in the years following the fall of the Sith Eternal Order. They are the first strike by the [Sith Dominion](https://app.legendkeeper.com/a/worlds/clmywn5qlc9u70842gmlc344a/clmz0wt3300ev033c44oejqx0) against the galaxy, causing chaos and mayhem to distract the galaxy from the actions of the Sith Dominion. The Dra’jn King is a member of the [Archassai](https://app.legendkeeper.com/a/worlds/clmywn5qlc9u70842gmlc344a/clmz0znge000y356mldwfz2h8), and has changed several times in the Dra’jn’s history. All Dra’jn arms, equipment, and vehicles are provided by the Dominion’s [Infinite Engine](https://app.legendkeeper.com/a/worlds/clmywn5qlc9u70842gmlc344a/clmz12ihq0013356mhovhbs70).

# Dra'jn Equipment

The Dra’jn seemingly have their own means of equipping their forces with armaments and vehicles. Dra’jn troopers are clad in green plasti-steel armor and employ their own variants of blaster rifles, pistols, and cannons. Most Dra’jn troopers are skilled in close-quarters combat, employing a variety of batons, blades, and energized melee weapons, as well as open-hand combat styles.

Dra’jn ground forces deploy in small- and medium-sized personal transports. Additionally, Dra’jn utilizes armored tanks and combat walkers in various weight classes. While deployed in larger ground operations, Dra’jn forces are coordinated from battle domes \-- large, heavily armed mobile fortresses capable of housing hundreds of troops and dozens of ground vehicles. 

Dra’jn space forces are dominated by light interceptor fighters, medium range heavy bombers, and swift combat corvettes. Transport is provided by large, teardrop-shaped transports that utilize Dra’jn battle domes as primary bridges. Dra’jn’s navy centers around their pyramid-shaped light battlecruisers. While on the smaller size compared to similar warships of their class, these warships carry a variety of turbolasers and missile launchers, and can take on enemy warships well above their weight-class.
