# Free Commerce Consortium

The Free Commerce Consortium is a collection of worlds controlled by a cadre of intergalactic corporations. The Consortium is effectively a corporate democracy, where representatives of thirty five different corporations meet on the “boardworld” of Kriselist to make policy and elect leaders. Ironically, a few surviving Separatist corporations that survived the Clone Wars have joined the Consortium, achieving the political and economic independence their forbearers had sought. 

The Consortium maintains mostly-positive relations with the other galactic powers. Consortium worlds produce significant amounts of consumer goods and material used the galaxy over. They are also one of the leading producers of military grade weapons, electronics, and droids. This makes the Consortium one of the galaxy’s leading arms dealers, a fact that is not lost on those facing off against Techno-Union war droids or Czerka Arms equipped shocktroopers. 

Life in the Consortium is described by outsiders as “a capitalist dystopia”, with a clear divide between the wealthy corporates and the destitute and downtrodden. The ultra-wealthy fosters an economic class warfare to help maintain control and power. In spite of this, there are those who push back against capitalist doctrine and advocate for a more socialist society, in the form of self-funded welfare programs, activism, and in some cases acting against the corporations as provocateurs.
