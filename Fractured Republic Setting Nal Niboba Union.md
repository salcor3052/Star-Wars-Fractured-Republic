# Nal Niboba Union

![][image18]  
An ever-present power throughout galactic history, the Hutt Kajidics have prospered in the past hundred years. Their Cartels have taken advantage of the fractured political landscape by cozying up to several new galactic powers. By offering trade and labor to these fledgling powers, the Hutts have gained economic wealth and political might. Cartel transports move goods throughout the galaxy, protected by loyal (i.e. well-paid) mercenary forces. Their luxury resort worlds offer fine dining, entertainment, and high-stakes adventures to all (all who can pay, at any rate).

The Cartels have not completely “gone legit”, the Hutts are still the ultimate power of the galaxy’s criminal underworld. Spice running, smuggling, extortion, assassination, and all manners of vices can be found within Hutt Space. Anyone operating within Hutt space are beholden to one of the Kajidics, lest the offending organization risk the wrath of the entire Hutt Cartel coming down on  their heads.
