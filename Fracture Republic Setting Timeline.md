| 37 | Galactic | Corellia annoucnes the formation of the Corellian Alliance with Nubia, Sacorria, Plympto, and Lettow as founding member-planets. Worker uprisings have overthrown the prior ruling class and a new worker-elected government takes control. All worlds within the former Senex-Juvex sector are recognized members of the Confederation. All ships entering or leaving Confederacy space are subject to rigorous inspections for contraband and fugitives. Severe taxes are levied for any imported products to the Confederacy. |
